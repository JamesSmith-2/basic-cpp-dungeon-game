This is intended to be a text-based video game similar to the idea of a 5-room dungeon in Dungeons and Dragons
Beginning with the character selection and player integration
Then into a small cutscene of the adventurers walking into the dungeon and entering the first room
Print out the description of the first room
Then, randomly select one of three other rooms
Enter and describe those rooms
Two of which will have combat, (one small and one medium)
One will be a rest and Looting room
Then enter the boss room

(Combat akin to Undertale, text-based)

Combat with the boss
Every room will have multiple interactable things, such as a wardrobe or chests.
